BYU Propulsion Research Team

  • As part of the BYU propulsion research team, I work to develop a hybrid rocket engine and increase the thrust to 600 lb to integrate it into a high-powered rocket and launch it at a competition.
  • The rocket engine has successfully completed a static fire campaign where it achieved our target thrust of 500 lbs and surpassed it by 24%. That margin gives us the go-ahead to launch the hybrid engine by the end of this year at FAR in California.
  • I work on the fluids system for block 3, a bigger and more powerful hybrid rocket engine. I sized the plumbing for increased flow and modeled the new system in CAD.
  • To move to the next engine, we will build a Mojave Sphinx style liquid rocket engine. As we integrate and grow, I keep learning with the university team on the way toward an attempted space shot.
