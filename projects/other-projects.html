Other Projects

I always have a project in motion and more goals lined up behind it.

In progress

  1. I put together a team of BYU students for the Boom supersonic challenge: design and build a 25 kg, manually controlled RC plane that takes off and holds at least Mach 1, climbing or level, for a minimum of 5 seconds — then do it twice in the same day with minimum repairs. The hard parts include FAA rules for supersonic flight, modifying a turbojet to work at Mach 1, a composite airframe light enough and strong enough for the transonic region without wing flutter or failure from aerodynamic heating, and avionics that can control the plane at Mach 1.
  2. I am nearly done with an I-class model rocket aimed at 7,000 feet and Mach 1.2, built for an L1 amateur rocketry certificate. After that I will build a larger rocket for L2.
  3. I have researched and designed a small water-cooled rocket engine that runs on liquid propane and gaseous oxygen. The calculations look on spec. Next is a prototype injector plate, then build and test. A main challenge is controlling liquid propane at 200 psi and gaseous oxygen at 400 psi so they mix at the right ratio and stay safe.

Planned

  1. A small centrifugal-compressor turbojet built around an automobile turbocharger. I already have a few turbos and want the experience of making turbomachinery into a working jet.
  2. A rotating detonation engine, once I have more experience. I have researched it and built a rough CAD model. Before any hardware I need more research and the performance calculations. The hardest parts will be fuel and oxidizer control, and a pre-detonator with working electronics.

There are other projects behind these that shaped how I work. I hope this portfolio shows the goals I can reach when I commit to them. More information and references are available on request.
