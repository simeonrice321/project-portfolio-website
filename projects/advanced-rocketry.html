Advanced Rocketry — Centurion

  • I helped lead and guide a BYU rocketry team to win a university competition with our rocket, Centurion.
  • The project was a chance to grow leadership skills. I connected with the team as we designed and built a 5-foot rocket that outperformed our expectations.
  • The main challenge was calculating exactly how high the rocket would go, and trying to reach 4,000 feet. To win, I worked through calculations and design changes with the team so we hit the weight and specifications we needed.
  • The rocket reached 4,133 feet — the closest to the goal of all the university teams. We also gathered telemetry on speed and acceleration. The rocket pulled 21 Gs with a max speed of Mach 0.95.
  • Launching something I helped design and build, and watching it go up straight, taught me more than any project before it.
